Uttarakhand GK Questions
========================

1. When was Uttarakhand carved out of Uttar Pradesh to become a separate state?
Answer: November 9, 2000.

2. What are the official capitals of Uttarakhand?
Answer: Dehradun (Winter) and Gairsain (Summer).

3. What is the total geographical area of Uttarakhand?
Answer: 53,483 sq. km.

4. How many administrative districts does Uttarakhand have?
Answer: 13 districts (divided into Garhwal and Kumaon divisions).

5. Which is the largest district in Uttarakhand by area?
Answer: Chamoli.

6. Which is the smallest district in Uttarakhand by area?
Answer: Champawat.

7. Which Indian states share borders with Uttarakhand?
Answer: Himachal Pradesh (North-West) and Uttar Pradesh (South).

8. Which international borders does the state share?
Answer: Nepal (East) and Tibet/China (North).

9. Which is the highest mountain peak in Uttarakhand?
Answer: Nanda Devi (7,816 meters).

10. At which exact place do the Bhagirathi and Alaknanda rivers meet to form the Ganga?
Answer: Devprayag.

11. Where does the Alaknanda River originate?
Answer: Satopanth Glacier.

12. Where does the Bhagirathi River originate?
Answer: Gaumukh (Gangotri Glacier).

13. Which river forms the natural eastern boundary between Uttarakhand and Nepal?
Answer: Sharda (Kali) River.

14. Which river is primarily responsible for forming the Doon Valley?
Answer: Song River (along with Asan and Suswa).

15. Which lake in Uttarakhand is famous as the "Mystery Lake" or "Skeleton Lake"?
Answer: Roopkund Lake.

Answer: Nainital.

Answer: Lipulekh Pass.

Answer: Bhilangana River.

Answer: Mana (Chamoli district).
